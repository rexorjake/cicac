How to Sync iPads, iPhones & Androids

  1. Open your GitHub Pages link on Device 1: Your 4-digit Room Code starts hosting automatically.
  2. Open the same link on Device 2: Switch to Join Room, enter the 4-digit code (or scan the QR code), and tap Join.
  3. Clock-drift-free sync: SyncTimer sends exact remaining duration (not raw device clock timestamps) and queues every Start, Pause, and Reset in strict order so both devices match even if their system clocks differ.
Tip: Tap the Sun icon on your Display device to keep the screen awake, and tap the Speaker icon once to enable chime audio.
